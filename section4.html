Testimonial · 流通分

佛说阿弥陀经

← 第三章 目录 Contents

舍利弗，如我今者，称赞诸佛不可思议功德，彼诸佛等，亦称赞我不可思议功德，而作是言：「释迦牟尼佛，能为甚难希有之事，能于娑婆国土，五浊恶世——劫浊、见浊、烦恼浊、众生浊、命浊中——得阿耨多罗三藐三菩提；为诸众生，说是一切世间难信之法。」舍利弗，当知我于五浊恶世，行此难事，得阿耨多罗三藐三菩提，为一切世间说此难信之法，是为甚难。佛说此经已，舍利弗，及诸比丘，并一切世间，天、人、阿修罗等，闻佛所说，欢喜信受，作礼而去。

逐句解释

如我今者，称赞诸佛不可思议功德，彼诸佛等，亦称赞我不可思议功德

就像我现在称赞十方诸佛不可思议的功德，那些诸佛也同样称赞我释迦牟尼佛不可思议的功德。这是一种相互印证：释迦牟尼佛赞叹阿弥陀佛及十方诸佛，十方诸佛也赞叹释迦牟尼佛。这种相互称赞说明，这个教法是一切佛共同认可的，不是某一位佛单独宣说的特殊教法。

Just as I now praise the inconceivable merits of the Buddhas, those Buddhas also praise my inconceivable merits

This mutual praise is significant. The Amitabha Sutra includes a lengthy passage (the Six Directions section, found in the full Kumarajiva text before this closing) where the Buddhas of all six directions extend their tongues to cover their world-systems and testify to the truth of this teaching. Here Shakyamuni confirms the reciprocity: he praises them, and they praise him. This cross-validation across all Buddhas establishes the Amitabha Sutra as a teaching authenticated not by one voice but by the unanimous witness of all enlightened beings across all worlds. In the Buddhist tradition, such universal consensus is the highest possible confirmation of a teaching's truth and value.

释迦牟尼佛，能为甚难希有之事，能于娑婆国土，五浊恶世中，得阿耨多罗三藐三菩提

诸佛称赞说：释迦牟尼佛能做极难、极为稀有的事——他在我们这个「娑婆」世界（梵文 Sahā，意为「堪忍」，即众生能够忍受其中苦难的世界）、在五浊恶世中证得无上正等正觉（阿耨多罗三藐三菩提）。「五浊」是这个时代的五种染污：劫浊（时代的污浊）、见浊（邪见盛行）、烦恼浊（贪嗔痴盛行）、众生浊（众生根器低劣）、命浊（寿命短促）。

Shakyamuni Buddha was able to accomplish something extremely difficult and rare — to attain unsurpassed, complete, and perfect enlightenment in the Saha world, in this age of the five defilements

Saha (娑婆) means "endurance" — it is the world where beings endure suffering. Our world is defined by its difficulty. The five defilements (五浊) describe the compounding challenges of this era: the defilement of the age itself (劫浊, an era in which conditions for practice are generally poor); the defilement of views (见浊, the proliferation of wrong and confused ideas about reality); the defilement of afflictions (烦恼浊, the intensification of greed, hatred, and delusion); the defilement of beings (众生浊, the deterioration of human capacities and character); and the defilement of lifespan (命浊, the shortening of life leaving less time to practise). That Shakyamuni attained full enlightenment under these conditions is, from the perspective of all other Buddhas, an act of extraordinary courage and power. Anuttara-samyak-sambodhi (阿耨多罗三藐三菩提) is the Sanskrit term for unsurpassed, perfect enlightenment — the complete awakening of a fully realised Buddha.

为诸众生，说是一切世间难信之法

诸佛称赞释迦牟尼佛的第二件难事是：他为众生宣说了「一切世间难信之法」——这个世间极难相信的教法。这句话点出了净土法门最根本的特点：它难以置信。一个在极远处存在的完美世界，只要念佛七天就能往生——对于一般人而言，这确实难以相信。正因为难信，诸佛才要共同出来作证，说明这是真实不虚的。

And to speak for all beings this teaching that the whole world finds difficult to believe

This phrase — "difficult to believe" (难信之法) — is one of the most important in the entire sutra. The Pure Land teaching asks for a very particular kind of faith: trust that an inconceivably distant realm exists, that a Buddha named Amitabha is there right now, that sincere recitation of his name for as little as one day can result in rebirth there, and that all the other Buddhas in all directions unanimously confirm this. For a rational, sceptical mind, every part of this stretches credibility. The sutra does not pretend otherwise — it acknowledges directly that this is hard to believe. But the acknowledgement itself is an invitation: the difficulty of belief is precisely why it requires faith, and why faith, when it arises, is itself a form of merit. The teaching asks not for blind acceptance but for the courage to practise and see for oneself.

当知我于五浊恶世，行此难事，得阿耨多罗三藐三菩提，为一切世间说此难信之法，是为甚难

佛陀自己也肯定：在五浊恶世中成就无上菩提，并为世间宣说这个难信的法，这是极难的事。这是佛陀罕见地对自己成就的肯定，也是对一切修行者的鼓励：在这个艰难的时代，能听到、能信受这个法门，本身就是极大的福报和因缘。

Know that in this age of five defilements, I have accomplished this difficult task — attaining unsurpassed enlightenment and teaching this hard-to-believe Dharma to all the world. This is extremely difficult

Shakyamuni here does something rare in the sutras: he affirms his own achievement, not out of pride but to underscore the significance of what is being offered. He has done two extraordinarily difficult things: awakened in the most challenging of conditions, and then turned back to offer the most counter-intuitive and hardest-to-believe teaching to beings who are, by definition, living in an age of confusion. The implicit message to the listener is: if hearing this teaching is rare, and if believing it requires overcoming the doubts natural to a deluded age, then the fact that you are hearing it and feel moved to practise it is itself remarkable. It is an encouragement to take seriously what has been given.

佛说此经已，舍利弗，及诸比丘，并一切世间，天、人、阿修罗等，闻佛所说，欢喜信受，作礼而去

佛陀说完这部经后，舍利弗、诸比丘，以及一切世间的天众、人、阿修罗等，听了佛所说的，都欢喜信受，顶礼佛陀后离去。「欢喜信受」四个字是经典结尾的标准格式，表示整个大众都以喜悦和信心接受了这个教法，没有疑惑，没有争议。这也是一种示范：这就是听法的正确态度。

When the Buddha had finished teaching this sutra, Shariputra, the assembly of monks, and all the beings of the world — gods, humans, asuras, and others — joyfully received and believed what the Buddha had said, paid their respects, and departed

The closing formula "joyfully received and believed" (欢喜信受) is the standard ending for Mahayana sutras and is not merely formulaic here. Joy (欢喜) and faith (信受) together describe the ideal response to this teaching. Joy because the Pure Land path, once understood, is genuinely good news: there is a door out of suffering that is open to all, right now. Faith because the teaching asks for trust before it can be verified by direct experience. Together they form the disposition that makes the practice possible. The assembly bows and departs — not disappointed or confused, but settled, grateful, and ready to practise. The sutra ends as it began: with the entire cosmos gathered together, moved by what has been freely given.

总结 · Summary

流通分是这部经的结尾，也是对整部经意义的最终肯定。十方诸佛互相印证，称赞释迦牟尼佛在五浊恶世中成就菩提、宣说难信之法，是极难希有的事。佛陀自己也承认：这个法门难以置信，但正因为难信，才更显珍贵。最后，全场大众欢喜信受，作礼而去。这部经从「无问自说」开始，以「欢喜信受」结束，全程都是佛陀对众生无条件的悲悯与馈赠。

The testimonial section brings the sutra to its close with a final act of cosmic authentication. All Buddhas praise Shakyamuni for doing what is most difficult: awakening in an age of defilement and then offering, without being asked, a teaching that strains credibility but opens a door to liberation for all. The phrase "difficult to believe" is not a warning to doubt the teaching but an honest acknowledgement of what genuine faith requires — and an implicit honouring of all who, hearing this, choose to trust and practise. The sutra ends with the whole assembly receiving the teaching in joy. It began with the Buddha speaking freely out of compassion; it ends with beings responding in faith. That exchange — freely given, freely received — is itself a small image of the Pure Land.
