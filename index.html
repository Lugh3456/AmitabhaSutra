Amitabha Sutra

佛说阿弥陀经

Mahayana · Pure Land · 净土宗

The Amitabha Sutra (佛说阿弥陀经) is the foundational scripture of Pure Land Buddhism, one of the most widely practised forms of Buddhism in East Asia. Spoken directly by Shakyamuni Buddha without being asked, it describes the Western Pure Land (极乐世界) of Amitabha Buddha — a realm of perfect conditions for awakening — and teaches the practice of reciting Amitabha's name with single-minded devotion as the path to rebirth there. Translated into Chinese by Kumārajīva (鸠摩罗什) in 402 CE, this version remains the standard recitation text in Chinese Buddhist tradition.
