佛说阿弥陀经

← 目录 Contents 第二章 →

如是我闻：一时，佛在舍卫国祇树给孤独园，与大比丘僧，千二百五十人俱，皆是大阿罗汉，众所知识：长老舍利弗、摩诃目犍连、摩诃迦叶、摩诃迦旃延、摩诃俱絺罗、离婆多、周利槃陀伽、难陀、阿难陀、罗睺罗、憍梵波提、宾头卢颇罗堕、迦留陀夷、摩诃劫宾那、薄拘罗、阿㝹楼驮，如是等诸大弟子。并诸菩萨摩诃萨：文殊师利法王子、阿逸多菩萨、乾陀诃提菩萨、常精进菩萨，与如是等诸大菩萨。及释提桓因等，无量诸天大众俱。

逐句解释

如是我闻

「如是我闻」是佛经的传统开篇语，意思是「这是我亲耳听闻的」。「我」指的是阿难陀，佛陀最亲近的侍者，以记忆力超凡著称，负责在佛灭度后结集经典。这四个字是一种见证：说明这部经不是后人杜撰，而是有人亲历的教说。

Thus have I heard

These four words open almost every Buddhist sutra and serve as a seal of authenticity. "I" refers to Ananda (阿难陀), the Buddha's closest attendant for twenty-five years, renowned for his extraordinary memory. After the Buddha passed into final nirvana, Ananda recited all the discourses he had heard so they could be compiled into scripture. By opening with "thus have I heard," the sutra makes clear that what follows is not philosophy invented by later thinkers but a direct record of something spoken and witnessed. It is the ancient equivalent of a first-hand testimony.

一时，佛在舍卫国祇树给孤独园

「一时」意思是「在某个特定的时刻」。佛当时住在舍卫国的祇陀园，这座园林由长者给孤独（须达多）以大量黄金铺地买下，献给佛陀作为讲法的道场，是佛陀最常驻锡和说法的地方之一。

At one time, the Buddha was staying in the Jeta Grove, Anathapindika's Garden, in the land of Shravasti

Shravasti (舍卫国) was a major city in ancient India, capital of the Kosala kingdom. The Jeta Grove (祇树) belonged to Prince Jeta, while Anathapindika (给孤独, literally "feeder of the destitute") — a wealthy merchant named Sudatta — purchased the surrounding land from the prince by covering it entirely with gold coins, then donated the whole estate to the Buddha as a permanent residence. This garden became one of the most important centres of the early Buddhist community, where the Buddha gave more teachings than at any other location. It is the setting for many of the most famous sutras.

与大比丘僧，千二百五十人俱，皆是大阿罗汉，众所知识

佛陀与一千二百五十位比丘（出家男众）同在。这个数字在佛经中反复出现，因为它代表了最初皈依佛陀的几个主要弟子群体的总和。这些比丘都已证得阿罗汉果——断尽烦恼、了生脱死的圣者——且都是众所周知、广受尊敬的人。

Together with a great assembly of monks, twelve hundred and fifty in all, every one of them a great Arhat, known and esteemed by all

The number 1,250 is not arbitrary. It represents the combined total of several major groups who took refuge with the Buddha early in his ministry: the three Kashyapa brothers and their followers (1,000 monks), Shariputra and Maudgalyayana with their followers (200 monks), and Yasha with his companions (50 monks). An Arhat (阿罗汉) is one who has completely eradicated all defilements — greed, hatred, and delusion — and will not be reborn again in the cycle of suffering. This is the highest attainment in the early Buddhist path. The phrase "known and esteemed by all" signals that these are not ordinary monastics but the most celebrated spiritual masters of their time.

长老舍利弗、摩诃目犍连、摩诃迦叶、摩诃迦旃延、摩诃俱絺罗、离婆多、周利槃陀伽、难陀、阿难陀、罗睺罗、憍梵波提、宾头卢颇罗堕、迦留陀夷、摩诃劫宾那、薄拘罗、阿㝹楼驮，如是等诸大弟子

经文点名列出了十六位最著名的大弟子。舍利弗是智慧第一，目犍连是神通第一，迦叶是头陀苦行第一，阿难陀是多闻第一，罗睺罗是密行第一。每一位都在某个方面代表着修行的高峰。

The Elder Shariputra, Mahā-Maudgalyāyana, Mahā-Kāshyapa, Mahā-Kātyāyana, Mahā-Kausthila, Revata, Shuddhipanthaka, Nanda, Ānanda, Rāhula, Gavāmpati, Pindola-Bhāradvāja, Kālodāyin, Mahā-Kapphina, Vakkula, Aniruddha — these and other great disciples

The sixteen named disciples each excelled in a particular quality. Shariputra (舍利弗) was foremost in wisdom; Maudgalyayana (目犍连) in supernatural powers; Mahakashyapa (迦叶) in ascetic discipline; Ananda (阿难陀) in learning and memory; Rahula (罗睺罗), the Buddha's own son, in disciplined practice. Shuddhipanthaka (周利槃陀伽) was famously slow to learn but attained enlightenment through persistent effort — a source of encouragement to all practitioners. Aniruddha (阿㝹楼驮), having gone blind, developed the divine eye and could see across worlds. Together they represent the full spectrum of how the path can be walked and where it leads.

并诸菩萨摩诃萨：文殊师利法王子、阿逸多菩萨、乾陀诃提菩萨、常精进菩萨，与如是等诸大菩萨

大乘经典中，除了声闻弟子，还有菩萨出席。菩萨摩诃萨（大菩萨）是已发菩提心、立誓度化一切众生的大修行者。文殊菩萨代表般若智慧，阿逸多即弥勒菩萨，是未来佛。常精进菩萨顾名思义，象征永不退转的精进心。

And also the great Bodhisattvas: Mañjushrī, Prince of the Dharma; Bodhisattva Ajita; Bodhisattva Gandhahastin; Bodhisattva Nityodyukta — these and other great Bodhisattvas

This signals the Mahayana context of the sutra. While Arhats have attained personal liberation, Bodhisattvas (菩萨) have taken the vow to remain in the world until all beings are liberated — they are driven by compassion as much as wisdom. Manjushri (文殊师利) embodies transcendent wisdom and is often depicted wielding a sword that cuts through ignorance. Ajita (阿逸多) is another name for Maitreya — the future Buddha who is currently a Bodhisattva awaiting his turn to appear in our world. Nityodyukta (常精进, "ever-diligent") personifies the quality of tireless, unwavering effort on the path. Their presence establishes that the teaching about to be given is of the highest Mahayana significance.

及释提桓因等，无量诸天大众俱

出席的不只是人间的修行者，还有来自天界的无量天众，以释提桓因（即帝释天，忉利天的天主）为代表。这说明这次法会超越了人间，是贯穿欲界诸天的盛大集会。

And Shakra, Lord of the Gods, together with countless gods and other great beings

Shakra (释提桓因) is Indra — the king of the Trāyastrimsha heaven (忉利天), one of the desire-realm heavens in Buddhist cosmology. His presence, along with countless other celestial beings, elevates the assembly beyond a human gathering. In Buddhist understanding, there are many realms of existence, and beings from multiple realms may gather to hear the Dharma. This detail conveys the cosmic scope of what is about to be taught: it is not a local teaching for one group of people, but a universal message relevant to all sentient beings across many worlds. The Amitabha Sutra itself will describe a realm beyond even the heavens Shakra inhabits.

总结 · Summary

序分交代了这次说法的时间、地点和出席者。佛陀在舍卫国祇陀园，面对一千二百五十位已证阿罗汉果的比丘、数位大菩萨，以及无量天众，开始说法。值得注意的是，这部经是佛陀主动宣说的，没有人请法——在佛经中，这是极为罕见的，说明此法对众生极为重要，佛陀认为非说不可。

The introduction establishes the setting: the Buddha is at Jeta Grove in Shravasti, surrounded by 1,250 Arhat monks, several great Bodhisattvas, and innumerable celestial beings. What makes this sutra remarkable is that no one asks the Buddha to speak — he delivers this teaching entirely on his own initiative (无问自说). In the Buddhist tradition, this is highly unusual. Most sutras begin with a disciple requesting instruction. The fact that the Buddha volunteers this teaching without prompting signals its exceptional importance: he considers it so vital that it must be given whether or not anyone thinks to ask. The Pure Land teaching that follows is offered as a gift to all beings.
